Extracts

Railway Exfil

Open extract at the railway terminus, north\-west corner of the map\. Scav extract\.
Saferoom Exfil

Conditional extract\. Needs the power switch on \(Power Station\), the flush lever in the Burger Spot restroom on Mall Floor 2, and an Object \#11SR keycard swiped at the Saferoom door\. Single use — the keycard is consumed\. PMC extract\.
Railway Exfil

Open extract at the railway terminus, north\-west corner of the map\. PMC extract\.
Emercom Checkpoint

Open extract at the Emercom checkpoint, south\-east highway corner\. Shared extract\.
Scav Camp \(Co\-Op\)

Co\-op extract\. A PMC and a Scav must be in the extract zone at the same time for it to work\. Shared extract\.
Path to River \(Flare\)

Flare extract\. Fire a signal flare while standing in the marked zone to open the extract, then leave before the timer runs out\. PMC extract\.
Power Station V\-Ex

Vehicle extract\. Pay the driver in roubles; the car leaves when its timer ends and it can only be used once per raid\. PMC extract\.
Smugglers' Tunnel

Extract in the service tunnel beneath the mall — reach it from the parking garage level\. PMC extract\.
Hole in the Fence

No backpack allowed\. Drop your backpack before stepping in — you cannot extract while wearing one\. PMC extract\.
